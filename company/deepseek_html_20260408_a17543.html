Begin Trust Deed (Core)

Protect rural family land, own shares in ZANAQ Holdings, support beneficiaries (education, health, housing, business), preserve wealth across generations.

Land may never be sold, transferred, mortgaged or encumbered without unanimous written approval of all Trustees. Leases only to operating companies.

ZANAQ Holdings & Begin Masimba Farm

ZANAQ Holdings (Pvt) Ltd

100% owned by Begin Trust · Authorised shares: 10,000 · Issued: 1,000 ordinary shares.

Asset holding: livestock, greenhouses, equipment, IP, shares of Begin Masimba Farm.
Transfer restrictions Shares cannot be sold/pledged without unanimous Trustee + Board approval.

Protected matters: MOI amendment, share issuance, asset sale >$10k require unanimous trustee resolution.

Begin Masimba Farm (Pvt) Ltd

Operating company · 100% owned by ZANAQ Holdings. Responsible for poultry, piggery, greenhouses, crops, agro-processing, sales & distribution.

Land lease: 20-year renewable lease from Begin Trust at symbolic rent ($100/month). All improvements become trust property.

Operational liabilities stay inside this entity — land and holding company fully protected.

Land Lease Agreement

Lessor: Begin Trust  |  Lessee: Begin Masimba Farm (Pvt) Ltd

  • Term: 20 years + 20 year renewal option
  • Rent: USD $100 per month (quarterly in advance)
  • Improvements become Trust property upon installation
  • Sub‑lease prohibited without unanimous Trustee approval

Generational Wealth Succession Plan

Timeline & transition

  • Phase 1 (Years 1-15): Founder led — training successors.
  • Phase 2 (16-25): Co‑leadership — next generation assumes daily management.
  • Phase 3 (Year 26+): Next generation controls trust & companies.

Successor requirements

Deputy Managing Trustee for 3 years, financial literacy, farm work experience (18-25), unanimous trustee approval. No individual inherits trust assets directly → prevents fragmentation.

Contingency

Incapacity or death of Founder: interim managing trustee appointed by remaining trustees. Independent trustee has casting vote in disputes.

Review every 3 years by Trustees + Family Council
